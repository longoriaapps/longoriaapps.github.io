CONTACT SUPPORT

When you report a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Anemos version and build number, the type of ride you were recording, and a short description of what happened. If the issue involves location, weather, Auto-Pause, or a report, tell us when in the ride you noticed the problem.

Screenshots are useful when they do not contain routes, addresses, or other information you prefer to keep private.

GPS & LOCATION

Speed, distance, or route looks wrong

Anemos depends on iPhone location data. Confirm that Location Services are enabled for Anemos and that the phone has a usable GPS view of the sky. Buildings, heavy tree cover, terrain, signal conditions, and device placement can affect GPS accuracy.

GPS-derived speed and distance may fluctuate temporarily. They should not be treated as certified measurements.

WEATHER & WIND

Conditions are missing or different

Weather and wind information depend on current location, network availability, and the underlying weather service. Conditions at your exact position may differ from the nearest observation or forecast point.

For severe weather or safety decisions, use official weather warnings and local guidance.

AUTO-PAUSE

Ride paused or resumed unexpectedly

Auto-Pause uses movement information to decide when a ride appears to have stopped or resumed. Slow trail sections, GPS drift, brief stops, or poor signal conditions may occasionally affect that behavior.

You can use the manual pause control whenever you want direct control over recording.

BIKE PROFILES

Changing bikes or ride styles

Use bike profiles to keep ride settings aligned with the bicycle or riding style you plan to use. Review the selected profile before beginning a ride, especially after changing bicycles or customizing display options.

RIDE HISTORY

Completed ride is missing

Allow the ride to finish and save normally before closing Anemos. If a ride is interrupted by a device restart, force quit, storage problem, or other system event, the final record may be incomplete.

Ride history is stored locally on your iPhone, so deleting app data may also remove locally stored ride records.

REPORTS

Weekly or monthly totals

Anemos reports are calculated from the completed rides available in the app. If a ride is deleted, incomplete, or outside the selected reporting period, it may not be included in the displayed totals.

SPEED ALERTS

Personal alerts are not legal limits

Personal speed alerts are values you configure for your own awareness. They do not identify, verify, or replace posted legal speed limits, trail rules, or other official restrictions.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE

Keep attention on the ride

Anemos is an informational cycling tool. It is not a navigation system, legal speed-limit source, crash detector, or emergency service. Do not interact with the iPhone in a way that distracts you from traffic, trail conditions, pedestrians, other riders, obstacles, or changing weather.

In an emergency, use appropriate local emergency services and available safety resources rather than relying on Anemos.

PRIVACY

How Anemos handles ride information

For details about location, weather requests, local ride storage, permissions, and data retention, read the Anemos Privacy Policy.
