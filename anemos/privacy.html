ANEMOS RIDE INTELLIGENCE

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Anemos uses to provide cycling features, where ride information is stored, and what happens when Apple services are used.

Effective August 15, 2026

SUMMARY

Designed around local ride data

Anemos Ride Intelligence is designed as an informational cycling dashboard for iPhone. Ride information created by Anemos is stored locally on your iPhone. Longoria Apps does not operate a separate Anemos ride-history database.

LOCATION

GPS and location information

Anemos may request access to your iPhone location to calculate and display ride information such as GPS-based speed, distance, direction, elevation context, and route tracking.

You control Location Services permission through iOS. Limiting or disabling location access can prevent some ride features from working or reduce their accuracy.

WEATHER

Weather and wind information

Anemos may use your current or ride-area location to request weather conditions, advisories, and wind information from Apple-provided weather services. Those services may process the information needed to return weather results under Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Weather and wind information can be delayed, unavailable, or inaccurate and should be treated as informational.

RIDE RECORDS

Information you create

Ride records may include ride date and time, duration, distance, route, speed information, direction, elevation information, selected bike profile, pause status, and summary or report data derived from your rides.

These records remain on your iPhone unless you choose to remove them or take another action that causes the data to leave the device.

BIKE PROFILES

Your cycling preferences

Bike profiles, ride-display preferences, personal speed-alert settings, themes, and related configuration information are used to personalize Anemos on your device.

APPLE SERVICES

Platform services and permissions

Anemos relies on Apple platform technologies needed for features such as location, mapping or route presentation, and weather information. Apple may process information when those services are used. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's policies and your Apple device settings.

CONTROL

Permissions, retention, and deletion

You can review or change Anemos permissions in iOS Settings. Ride information stored by Anemos remains on your device until it is deleted through the app, removed as part of normal app-data management, or deleted when the app and its locally stored data are removed.

Removing the app does not necessarily remove information retained independently by Apple services, device backups, screenshots, exports, or other copies you created outside Anemos.

SAFETY & LIMITATIONS

Informational cycling data

Anemos is not a navigation system, a legal speed-limit source, a crash detector, or an emergency service. GPS, elevation, route, weather, wind, and other environmental information may vary in accuracy and availability. Do not rely on Anemos as a substitute for road signs, trail markings, official weather warnings, emergency services, or your own judgment.

CONTACT

Privacy questions

Questions about this policy or Anemos privacy practices can be sent to Longoria Apps.

support@longoriaapps.com
