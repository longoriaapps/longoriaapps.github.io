Location & ride data

Precise while you ride. Local afterward.

Anemos uses Location Services while you ride to calculate speed, distance, route, heading, elevation-related information, and location-based weather.

Completed rides and precise GPS routes may be stored locally on your device so you can review your ride history. They are not uploaded to Longoria Apps.

☁︎
Apple WeatherKit

Weather for where you are

Anemos supplies your current location to Apple WeatherKit when it requests local weather conditions or forecasts. Weather snapshots may be stored locally with completed rides.

Longoria Apps does not receive your location or WeatherKit request information.

♥
Apple Health

Workout integration, with your permission

Anemos may temporarily read body mass from HealthKit to estimate active cycling calories. The value is used for the calculation and is not copied into the Anemos ride database or transmitted to Longoria Apps.

When enabled, Anemos may write cycling workouts, cycling distance, active energy, workout routes, and related workout metadata to Apple Health.

Health information written to Apple Health is managed separately. Deleting a ride in Anemos does not automatically delete the corresponding Apple Health workout.
⚙︎
Bike information & preferences

Your setup stays with your setup

Bike details you enter, such as name, category, notes, wheel size, or estimated battery range, are stored locally on your device. Application preferences are stored locally as well.

Longoria Apps does not receive this information.

◎
Apple services

Apple frameworks provide core functionality

Anemos relies on Apple technologies including Core Location, HealthKit, WeatherKit, MapKit, and notifications.

Information handled directly by Apple services is governed by Apple's applicable privacy practices and terms.

What Anemos does not use

No advertising stack hiding behind the handlebars

Anemos does not use Longoria Apps user accounts, advertising networks, third-party analytics, third-party telemetry, cross-app tracking, advertising identifiers, data brokers, CloudKit synchronization, or Longoria Apps cloud storage for ride data.

Anemos does not sell personal information.

✦
Your control

Permissions remain yours

You control access to protected services such as Location and HealthKit through iOS permissions. Applicable permissions can be changed in device settings.

Locally stored rides and bike information can be deleted from within Anemos. Apple Health information can be reviewed and managed separately in the Health app.

⌫
Retention & deletion

Ride history stays until you remove it

Completed rides remain locally available until you delete them or remove the app. Removing Anemos removes its local app data subject to normal Apple device backup and restoration behavior.

Copies previously written to Apple Health have their own retention and deletion controls.

◌
Children

No developer database of rider profiles

Anemos is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Longoria Apps does not operate an Anemos account system or backend database containing rider profiles or ride histories.
