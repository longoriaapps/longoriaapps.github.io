KNOW WHAT ANEMOS IS

Ride intelligence, not an emergency system.

Anemos is an informational cycling tool. It is not a navigation system, a legal speed-limit source, a crash detector, or an emergency service. GPS, elevation, weather, wind, and other environmental information can vary in accuracy and availability.
