How MorpheusREM 1.4.0 handles Apple Health information, your dream journal, recordings, interpretation, and artwork.

Effective November 11, 2026

MORPHEUSREM

Your app and your choices

MorpheusREM helps you review sleep patterns and keep a dream journal. The app does not require a Longoria Apps account and contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics integrations. Longoria Apps does not sell personal data. Your journal and artwork are stored in the app on your device.

APPLE HEALTH

Sleep and wellness data

With your permission, MorpheusREM reads sleep stages, heart rate, and respiratory rate from Apple Health to show sleep summaries, history, and dream-window vitals. It does not write data to Apple Health or use Apple Health readings for advertising or marketing profiles. You can revoke access in iOS settings. The app does not include Apple Health samples in interpretation or artwork requests.

DREAM JOURNAL

Recordings, transcripts, and notes

When you record a dream, the app saves the audio in its local storage. The recording may remain there if transcription fails. Transcripts, notes, and saved interpretations are stored with the dream entry. The app uses Apple Speech Recognition to transcribe recordings after you grant permission. Transcription is not restricted to on-device processing, so Apple may process the audio using its speech recognition service.

LOCAL INTERPRETATION

Dictionary and Apple On-Device

Dictionary interpretation runs locally. On supported Apple Intelligence devices, Apple On-Device interprets the transcript and notes using Apple’s on-device model. These modes do not need an API key or send dream text to external AI providers.

OPTIONAL CLOUD AI

Use your own provider key

If you choose Cloud AI and confirm, MorpheusREM sends your transcript and notes to your selected provider: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or Google (Gemini). Your key is stored in the device Keychain and used to authenticate with that provider. Processing, retention, and deletion of submitted content are governed by the provider’s terms, privacy practices, and your account settings.

DREAMCANVAS

Apple Image Playground

DreamCanvas opens Apple Image Playground only after you choose to share the reviewed excerpt. Apple’s high-quality image generation uses Private Cloud Compute. The app saves artwork you accept and its descriptive information locally. DreamCanvas does not use a third-party image provider or require an API key.

STORAGE AND BACKUPS

Local data and device backups

MorpheusREM does not provide cross-device journal sync. Local recordings, journal entries, and saved artwork may be included in iCloud or computer backups depending on your device settings. Backups and any copies you share are managed separately from the app.

YOUR CHOICES

Editing and deletion

You can edit supported journal fields and delete individual artworks in Saved Artwork. This version has no individual delete control for journal entries or recordings. Deleting the app removes its local app container. It does not delete Apple Health records, existing backups, or data previously sent to another service.

To remove a provider key from this device, clear it in Settings before uninstalling. Revoke the key through the provider if you want it to stop working. Uninstalling the app is not a reliable way to clear a Keychain credential.

SUPPORT

Information you choose to send

If you email support, Longoria Apps receives what you include. Describe the issue and share only the information needed to resolve it. Please leave out API keys and unnecessary health or dream details.

AUDIENCE

General audience

MorpheusREM is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children.

WELLNESS NOTICE

Personal reflection and wellness

MorpheusREM is intended for personal reflection and wellness. It does not diagnose or treat health conditions.
