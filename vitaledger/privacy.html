VitaLedger Privacy

Your financial records
stay on your device.

This policy explains how VitaLedger handles the budgets, transactions, savings goals, investment records, and cash-holding information you enter.

Privacy QuestionsReturn to VitaLedger

Effective August 13, 2026

OUR COMMITMENT

Private by design, not by promise

VitaLedger is built so your financial recordkeeping stays under your control. It does not connect to your bank or brokerage, does not sell your financial information, and does not require a separate VitaLedger account.

NO ACCOUNT LINKS

No bank or brokerage credentials

VitaLedger never asks for online banking or brokerage login credentials. Balances, transactions, stock lots, and cash holdings are entered and maintained by you, not retrieved through an account connection.

LOCAL RECORDS

Records you enter

Income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, recurring expenses, stock lots, current prices, and cash-holding details are stored locally on your device using Apple-provided storage.

DEVICE AUTHENTICATION

Optional Face ID / Touch ID

You may enable optional device authentication for an added layer of protection over investment information. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never seen or stored by Longoria Apps.

ESTIMATES

Calculated figures

Cost basis, unrealized gains or losses, projected interest, and maturity values are calculated locally from the information you provide. These figures are estimates for personal planning, not official financial-institution records.

CSV IMPORT

Stock lot import

When you import a supported CSV file of stock lots, the file is read on your device to populate your records. VitaLedger does not transmit imported files to a Longoria Apps server.

LOCALIZATION

Spanish language support

VitaLedger supports Spanish localization for its interface. Enabling a language does not change how your financial records are stored or shared.

SHARING

Actions you initiate

When you export, copy, print, or share a report, transaction list, or investment summary, the destination app, service, or person may receive that information. Review exported material before sharing it.

CONTROL

Retention & deletion

Records remain in VitaLedger until you delete them. Deleting the app may not automatically remove information retained in device backups, exported files, or copies you previously shared.

VitaLedger does not connect to external financial-account systems, so there is no third-party account from which to separately delete synced data.
APPLE SERVICES

Platform storage

VitaLedger relies on Apple technologies for on-device storage and optional biometric authentication. Apple handles that functionality under its own terms and privacy policies.

CHILDREN

Children's privacy

VitaLedger is not directed to children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a Longoria Apps account or advertising system.

NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE

Personal recordkeeping only

VitaLedger is intended for personal organization, planning, and recordkeeping. Investment, interest, gain/loss, and tax-related figures should be confirmed against official records or with a qualified professional.

CONTACT

Questions or requests
